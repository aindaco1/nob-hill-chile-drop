**Nob Hill Chile Drop: Hot Spot check-in**

**December 31, 2026 · Hotel Zazz · Opens at 10 p.m. Mountain Time**

We’re checking in whole groups. One QR covers everyone on that pass, and they all need to enter together.

**Before your shift**

Open [volunteer check-in](https://aindaco1.github.io/nob-hill-chile-drop/check-in/) on your phone. Sign in with your organizer-approved email, then open the emailed link in the browser you’ll use for scanning. Keep that tab open and allow camera access. You’ll need a charged phone and an internet connection.

Ask an organizer for the reservation cutoff time and who’s coordinating space at the door.

**Checking in a group**

1. Ask for the group’s QR from their results email or pass page.
2. Tap **Scan a group QR** and point your camera at it. The result card will come into view automatically.
3. Confirm the **name, email and group size** with the guest. Make sure everyone is there. If someone’s still on the way, ask the group to wait.
4. Read the status below. If the group can enter, check both boxes: everyone is present, and there’s room for the whole party. Tap **Check in all N people** or **Admit waitlisted group of N**.
5. Wait for **“Checked in N people.”** Then give everyone a wristband and let the group enter together. Tap **Scan a group QR** for the next group.

**What the result means**

- **Valid admission QR:** This group was selected. Their spaces are held until the reservation cutoff. After that, admission depends on available space.
- **Valid waitlist QR:** Admit them only after the cutoff, when the page allows check-in and door staff confirm there’s room. A waitlist QR doesn’t reserve space. They’re still welcome to watch from the public viewing areas.
- **Already checked in:** Don’t check them in again or issue another set of wristbands. Returning guests use their wristbands, subject to available space.

**If something goes wrong**

Get an organizer if a QR is missing, unreadable or invalid, the guest details don’t match, a wristband is lost, or check-in is closed. Wait for their help before admitting the group.

If the connection drops during check-in, reconnect and scan the same QR again. The first attempt may have saved. Confirm the result before letting anyone in.

Sign out when your shift ends or before handing over your phone. Keep guest details and your sign-in link private.
