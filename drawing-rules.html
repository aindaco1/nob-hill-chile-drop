Hot Spot group admission

The free drawing.

Entry and selected admission are free. No purchase, donation, or payment information is required. An entry is not a confirmed reservation.

One entry, one group

Enter your name, email, and the number of people in your group, including yourself. Confirm your email using the link we send. Only verified group entries are eligible, and each email address can hold one group entry. You can update your group through its private link while entry remains open.

One random order. Whole groups.

Every verified group entry receives one place in a random order, regardless of group size. Organizers select whole groups in that order, up to a maximum of 2,000 guests. Selection stops when the next group will not fit. That group and all groups after it stay on the waitlist. This can leave some capacity unused; smaller groups do not skip ahead.

Closing and results

Organizers will close entry and run the drawing manually. The drawing date has not been announced. Entry changes close before the random order is prepared. Organizers review the result and publish it once; selected groups are notified by email. Every group can check its result through its private entry link. No guest names or emails appear in public results.

What selected admission includes

Selected groups receive admission to the Hot Spot at Hotel Zazz, with alcohol sales, music, and a close-up view of the chile drop. The Hot Spot opens at {{ site.data.event.access.opens_label }}; alcohol sales stop at {{ site.data.event.access.alcohol_ends_label }} Mountain Time. Drinks and other purchases are separate. Event coverage may include the area. Final arrival instructions will be announced before the event.

Public viewing

You can also watch the drop from public vantage points outside the Hot Spot without entering the drawing. The viewing map and street access details will be shared before the event.
