Door check-in

The counter records initial admissions, including waitlisted groups admitted at the door. Staff assess actual space separately; departures and wristband re-entry do not change this total.

After the cutoff, unused selected reservations are released and all new admissions depend on available space. The guest pass shows the current cutoff; changing it does not send an update email.

Volunteer access

Approved volunteers can verify group details and check groups in. They cannot run the drawing, export entries, change settings or manage volunteers. Adding an email does not send a message; volunteers request their own sign-in link.
