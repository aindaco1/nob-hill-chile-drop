Help keep the celebration safe and welcoming. Share these rules and the recording notice with everyone in your group before attending.

Hot Spot admission covers the designated event area, not hotel rooms or guest-only areas. Arrival, parking, street access and accessibility details will be posted before the event.

Respect the venue and each other

These attendee rules adapt the relevant Hotel Zazz policies:

  • Follow staff directions. Threats, damage, unlawful activity, disruptive intoxication and disturbance of hotel guests can result in refused admission or removal, consistent with applicable law. You are responsible for damage you cause.
  • Parents, guardians and chaperones must supervise children 12 and under. Do not run or gather in hotel hallways.
  • No smoking or vaping, including tobacco, cannabis, e-cigarettes and hookahs. Do not burn incense or diffuse scented oils.
  • No fireworks, candles, open flames, grills, explosives or hazardous substances. Report emergencies to staff and keep exits clear. Do not tamper with alarms or firefighting equipment.
  • The pool has no lifeguard. Respect event access boundaries around it.
  • No firearms or weapons, including licensed open or concealed carry. The hotel's exception covers on-duty law enforcement and designated military personnel required to carry them.
  • No pets. Service animals are welcome.
  • Look after your belongings; the hotel disclaims responsibility for loss, theft or damage. Vehicles and their contents are parked at the owner's risk. Admission does not reserve parking.
  • Personal photos must respect other guests and private events. Arrange separate photoshoots or filming with the hotel.

For lost property, contact Hotel Zazz at 505-266-7663 or stay@hotelzazz.com.
