Privacy

The free drawing collects your name, email address, group size, and agreement to the drawing rules. Organizers use this information to verify entries, run the drawing, contact selected groups, and administer admission. It is not a marketing signup.

Storage and email

The entry service runs on Cloudflare and stores entries in its D1 database. Resend delivers verification links, private entry links, results, and administrative reports. Abuse prevention uses hashed request identifiers. Administrative access is restricted; public results do not show guest names or email addresses.

Your private entry link grants access to your group details. Keep it private. Session access is stored only for the current browser tab and can be cleared by signing out or closing the tab.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages serves this website and may process technical information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. Links to Instagram and Google Maps take you to those services. The site has no advertising or analytics cookies; fonts and artwork are hosted with the site.

Event video

If an event video is added, its YouTube player loads only when you choose to play it. Playing it connects your browser to YouTube.

Questions, corrections, and deletion

You can update your name and group size through your private link while entry is open. For privacy questions or a request to delete your entry, contact alonso@dustwave.xyz.
