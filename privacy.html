Nob Hill Chile Drop

Privacy

The free drawing collects your name, email address, city, state/province, country, group size, agreement to the drawing and Hot Spot rules, and the version and time of your recording consent. Organizers use this information to verify entries, run the drawing, notify selected and waitlisted groups, and administer admission. It is not a marketing signup.

Storage and email

The entry service runs on Cloudflare and stores entries in its D1 database. Resend delivers verification links, private entry links, results, and administrative reports. Abuse prevention uses hashed request identifiers. Administrative access is restricted; public results do not show guest names or email addresses.

Your private entry link grants access to your group details. Keep it private. Session access is stored only for the current browser tab and can be cleared by signing out or closing the tab.

Group QR and volunteer check-in

Approved volunteers can use your QR to see your name, email, group size, selected/waitlisted status, and prior check-in. Scanning does not check you in; an authorized volunteer must confirm admission. We record the group size, check-in time and volunteer responsible. Organizers can review these records and manage volunteer access.

Keep your group QR private. Its link contains a signed group identifier, not your name or email. Anyone holding it can view the group pass and admission status; full contact details require owner or staff access. The pass link may be kept for the current browser tab so it remains available after a refresh. Volunteer sign-in is limited to approved emails and can be revoked. Camera frames are processed on the volunteer's device to read the QR and are not uploaded or retained.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages serves this website and may process technical information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. Links to Instagram and Google Maps take you to those services. The map in Getting here loads from OpenStreetMap as you approach that section and shares technical information such as your IP address with OpenStreetMap. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. The site has no advertising or analytics cookies; fonts and artwork are hosted with the site.

Recording consent and event video

The Hot Spot may be photographed, filmed, livestreamed or otherwise recorded for event advertising, marketing and promotion. Read the recording notice agreed to in the entry form. That agreement is separate from email updates; entering is not an email marketing signup.

When organizers enable the Channel 26 stream on the homepage, the embedded player connects your browser to the City of Albuquerque’s Cablecast streaming service, which may process technical information such as your IP address. Switching back to the animation removes the player.

If an event video is added, its YouTube player loads only when you choose to play it. Playing it connects your browser to YouTube.

Questions, corrections, and deletion

You can update your name, city, state/province, country and group size through your private link while entry is open. For privacy questions or a request to delete your entry, contact alonso@dustwave.xyz.
